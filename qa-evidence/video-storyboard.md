# Demo video storyboard

Record with a clean synthetic account. Do not show browser developer tools, addresses containing
tokens, local-storage contents, or real medical data.

## Video 01 — Patient journey (90–120 seconds)

1. Landing page: explain the clinical safety positioning.
2. Registration: create a synthetic patient.
3. Dashboard: point out the workspace summary and assessment CTA.
4. Questionnaire: answer three or four questions and show progress/keyboard support.
5. Diagnosis: show evidence, triage guidance, and physician-review status.
6. Health report: show timeline and print action.
7. Sign out and demonstrate protected-route redirect.

## Video 02 — Clinician review (60–90 seconds)

1. Log in as the seeded demo clinician.
2. Open Patient Triage.
3. Review a synthetic submission.
4. Approve or correct it.
5. Open Clinical Metrics and show the updated review counts.

## Export settings

- 1080p, 30fps, MP4 (H.264) or WebM.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%.
- Capture desktop and one 390px mobile clip.
- Blur or crop any email address shown in the UI.
